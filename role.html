Product & Design

Product Designer

  • Frame product problems using user, business and technical context.
  • Design end-to-end workflows, from early concepts to detailed interaction states.
  • Build and maintain reusable patterns with engineering partners.
  • Use feedback, usability evidence and product signals to improve shipped work.
  • Communicate rationale clearly and take responsibility for design quality through delivery.
  • A portfolio that explains your role, process, decisions and outcomes without overstating impact.
  • Experience designing workflow-heavy web products or comparable digital services.
  • Strong information architecture, interaction design and visual design fundamentals.
  • Comfort working through ambiguity with cross-functional teammates.
  • Clear written and verbal communication, including constructive design critique.
  • Experience with enterprise SaaS, education operations or contract workflows.
  • Experience contributing to a design system and accessibility practices.
  • Familiarity with research planning, prototyping and product analytics.
  • How education and contract teams manage high-context operational work.
  • How to balance user needs, product strategy and implementation constraints.
  • How shared product patterns evolve across multiple enterprise products.
